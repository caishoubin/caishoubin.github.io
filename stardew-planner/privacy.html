Privacy

Your plan stays in your browser.

A small tool should need very little from you. Last Seed does not ask for your name, email, game account, or save file.

Last reviewed October 21, 2026 · Independent fan resource

Calculator inputs

The season, planting day, gold budget, plot count, and selected crop are processed by JavaScript in your browser. The calculator does not send those inputs to an application server. With “Remember on this device” enabled, it saves your last valid plan and comparison settings in this browser’s local storage. Unfinished or invalid edits are not saved. Turn that option off to remove the saved plan inputs; only the off preference is retained. Reset plan restores the default inputs. Browser storage can be cleared through your browser’s site-data settings. Storage may be unavailable in restricted or private browsing, and plans are not synchronized between devices.

Share links and CSV exports

“Copy plan link” creates a URL with the season, crop, day, budget, plots, and comparison settings after the # symbol (the fragment). The calculator does not put these values in an HTTP query. Browsers do not send the fragment in the page request or HTTP referrer, but anyone with the complete link can read the inputs. Links may remain in browser history, clipboard history, or the app where you share them. Do not include anything private. A valid shared plan is previewed instead of the saved plan when opened; invalid links are ignored. Loading or previewing a plan does not overwrite saved data. A valid edit, or explicitly enabling remembering, saves the displayed plan when remembering is on.

CSV files are generated locally and contain the displayed comparison rows, inputs, source links, and calculation assumptions. The site does not upload those files. Sharing a file or link elsewhere is your choice.

Hosting and ordinary requests

This is a static website hosted with GitHub Pages. Opening a page requests its HTML, styles, scripts, and artwork from the hosting service. GitHub may process ordinary request information, such as your IP address, browser information, and access logs, under its own policies. Read the GitHub General Privacy Statement for the hosting provider’s practices. We do not have access to or control over every host-level log.

Cookies, analytics, and advertising

The current version includes no analytics service, advertising-serving JavaScript, external font service, embedded social widgets, or tracking cookies. An AdSense publisher-ownership meta tag is present in the page source. That tag alone does not load advertisements or an advertising tracking script, and it is not a claim of AdSense approval.

If advertising or analytics is introduced, this notice and any required consent controls should be updated before the new behavior is enabled. The description on this page applies to the version currently provided.

External links

Crop sources, the publisher’s GitHub profile, and other linked sites are separate services. When you choose to follow a link, their privacy practices apply. A browser may send ordinary referrer information when following a link. The calculator does not include your entered budget or plot count in those links.

Questions and source review

The publisher is caishoubin on GitHub. The public website repository is the place to review its source and available contribution channels. This site does not advertise a dedicated support inbox or response guarantee.
