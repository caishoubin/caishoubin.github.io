About the project

A little help before a big change.

Before Hardmode is an independent field guide and browser-local planning tool for Terraria players.

3 min readUpdated October 20, 2026Independent guide

Why this guide exists

The moment before your first Wall of Flesh victory can turn into an overwhelming list of things you might do. This site narrows that list into a manageable set of checkpoints and explains which projects are optional. Its purpose is to help you choose a next step, not to impose one correct way to play.

The planner is free to use and does not need an account. It is maintained by the publisher at github.com/caishoubin. This is an unofficial fan resource, with no affiliation, endorsement, or partnership with Re-Logic. Terraria and associated game names belong to their respective owners.

How we approach the advice

Mechanical claims are checked against the official Terraria Wiki. Recommendations about preparation order, prioritization, storage, and rehearsal are editorial suggestions. We deliberately separate game mechanics from recommendations and optional projects in the checklist.

The guides use original wording and original vector illustrations. No game sprites, screenshots, wiki artwork, or game logos are used. Illustrations evoke an adventure field guide and are not representations of actual Terraria assets.

The content was reviewed on October 20, 2026 for modern unmodded Desktop, Console, and Mobile Terraria. It is not a comprehensive walkthrough. Legacy editions, special world seeds, mods, and future patches can behave differently. Follow the wiki’s version notes when a detail is important to your run.

References behind the checklist

  • Game progression: the broad progression context.
  • Wall of Flesh and fight strategies: the transition trigger, summoning, and fight preparation.
  • Biome spread: the difference between new biome generation and ongoing spread.
  • Crates and Altars: early Hardmode resource routes and version-sensitive advice.
  • Life Crystal, Molten Pickaxe, and Pylons: specific preparation mechanics.

Individual guides also link their references. The wiki is a living source; check it for changes after this site’s review date.
