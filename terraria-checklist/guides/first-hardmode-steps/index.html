After the Wall falls.

Turn the first victory into a manageable next session: secure the drops, inspect the world, and choose one upgrade route.

5 min readUpdated October 20, 2026Independent guide

Take a moment before your next goal

Your first Wall of Flesh victory changes that world to Hardmode. This is a progression change, separate from the world’s Classic, Expert, or Master difficulty setting. The change is permanent through normal gameplay, and the world now has new threats and opportunities.

Before chasing the next material, collect the boss drops and make sure your inventory has room. Then return to a safe place, deposit items, and look at what you actually received. A deliberate first trip home is a better starting point than carrying a full inventory into unfamiliar enemies.

Check what changed around your plans

Inspect your important settlements and travel links. The newly created biome regions can affect places you used comfortably before the fight. Check the actual world rather than assuming the new danger begins exactly where the old evil biome stopped.

Make your next expedition short. Take only the supplies you need, leave valuables at home, and give yourself a specific turnaround point. Your first goal can simply be learning which nearby routes are still comfortable.

If you play with others, compare priorities before splitting up. One player organizing supplies while another scouts can help, but only if both know where shared materials and drops will be stored.

Choose one early upgrade route

The mining route

The Pwnhammer from the Wall of Flesh can break Demon or Crimson Altars to generate Hardmode ores. The first three generate successive ore tiers. A Molten Pickaxe is the usual pre-Hardmode tool for starting that mining progression. Leave at least one convenient altar available for its crafting uses.

Plan the trip around one useful result rather than carrying everything you find until your inventory overflows. Once you have enough for the next step you intended, return and reassess. Better equipment is easier to appreciate when you can compare it directly with your current setup.

The fishing route

Fishing can provide an alternative source of Hardmode ore and bars through appropriate crates. The important timing is when the crate is caught: pre-Hardmode crates do not turn into their Hardmode equivalents after the transition. A saved stack of old crates is not a shortcut to those new materials.

Choose the route you enjoy and can carry out safely. You can combine methods; this is not a permanent character choice. The checklist treats a Molten Pickaxe as route-dependent advice for that reason.

Retire two outdated assumptions

  • “Every altar can create another random infected block.” On modern versions, the old random-block conversion behavior was removed in 1.4.4. Use the current altar article for your platform rather than applying older advice automatically.
  • “A completed checklist means I cannot struggle.” Preparation reduces avoidable friction. It does not remove the learning curve or make every new enemy safe.

Do not treat a difficult first outing as evidence that you prepared incorrectly. Identify the specific problem: lack of mobility, low damage, an awkward route, or supplies running out. Improve that one constraint before starting another broad preparation project.

Leave a clear starting point

Before ending the session, put the next supplies together and decide on one concrete objective. “Scout a short ore route” is easier to resume than “get ready for everything.” Keep interesting side projects in reserve instead of allowing them to compete with every immediate need.

The checklist can remain useful after the transition. It is fine to leave optional entries unfinished, export the record of this world, and start a fresh list when you begin another playthrough.
