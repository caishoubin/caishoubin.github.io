Checklist/Field notes
Using the planner

A checklist that fits your adventure.

Use the planner to choose your next useful step. Keep your progress in this browser, or carry it to another device with a small backup file.

4 min readUpdated October 20, 2026Independent guide

Start with one category

Open the checklist and choose Your character, Your world, Your supplies, or The final fight. The category buttons narrow the view; they do not change your saved progress. Use the search box to find a word or topic, then combine it with completion and advice-type filters.

Click a checkpoint or use its checkbox to mark it complete. Keyboard users can move through the controls with Tab and toggle a focused checkbox with Space. The count at the top always covers all 24 checkpoints, even when a filter hides some of them.

Understand the three labels

  • Recommended: practical preparation that often makes the transition more comfortable.
  • Optional: a useful project you can skip or leave for later.
  • Game mechanic: a rule or behavior worth understanding. Checking it means you have reviewed it, not that the website has detected anything in your game.

The percentage is just the fraction of checklist entries you marked complete. It is not a combat rating, a prediction, or a set of requirements imposed by Terraria. No game files are read, and nothing you do here changes your character or world.

Know where progress lives

When browser storage is available, each change is saved locally on this browser for this website address. Refreshing or returning later normally restores it. Progress does not automatically sync to other devices, different browsers, or a different website address.

Private browsing, clearing site data, browser policies, and storage limits can remove or prevent this saving. Check the save-status message near the checklist heading. If saving is blocked, the current page still works, but you should export before closing it.

There is one active checklist per browser profile and website origin. Other pages hosted on the same origin can technically access that origin’s browser storage. Do not treat local storage as an encrypted vault. This planner stores only checklist identifiers, not passwords or game saves.

Export, import, and start again

Export progress downloads a small JSON file containing the completed checkpoint identifiers and the backup format version. Keep the file on your device, or transfer it yourself to another device. You can rename the file to remember which world it belongs to.

Import progress reads a file you choose on your device. It checks the expected format and rejects unknown checklist entries or files larger than 64 KB. You will be asked to confirm before the current checklist is replaced. Import replaces progress rather than merging two worlds.

Reset checklist asks for confirmation before clearing the list. Export first if you want a way back. An export is a backup of this planner only; it is not a Terraria world or character backup.

Use the guides as a starting point

These guides focus on ordinary, unmodded modern Terraria worlds. Special seeds, mods, older platform editions, difficulty settings, and personal strategies can change what is useful. Follow the linked official wiki references when exact mechanics matter.

You can print a guide with your browser’s print command. The checklist also has a simplified print layout. For a compact paper plan, filter to a category or unfinished tasks before printing.
