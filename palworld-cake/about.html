Cake & Company / Field notes

A field tool for a well-stocked base

Updated October 21, 2026 · Normal Cake reference snapshot

Cake & Company is a small, independent utility for turning a Palworld cake target into a practical ingredient plan.

Built around the pantry, not just the recipe

A recipe total is a useful starting point. This planner goes one step further: it deducts ready cakes, counts ingredients already owned, separates ready flour from unprocessed wheat and presents one gathering route at a time. It also identifies tied limiting ingredients rather than pretending there is always just one bottleneck.

It is designed to work on a phone beside the game, with editable whole-item counts, optional local saving and copyable snapshots. There is no build step, account or backend needed to calculate a batch.

Independent and intentionally limited

Only Normal Cake and its flour-milling conversion are included. This is not an all-items database, a breeding optimizer or an in-game automation tool. Recipe references and limitations are documented on the sources page; the actual calculation method is explained in the methodology.

Publisher and feedback

Published by caishoubin on GitHub as part of an independent collection of game-planning tools. If you notice a recipe change or calculation issue, include the expected result, the game build you are using and a reproducible plan. Do not include account credentials or private information.
