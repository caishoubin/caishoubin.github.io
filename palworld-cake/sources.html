Cake & Company / Field notes

A small recipe book, kept transparent

Updated October 21, 2026 · Normal Cake reference snapshot

This calculator intentionally covers one regular recipe. A narrow catalog makes it easier to explain exactly what goes into the numbers.

Normal Cake

5 flour + 8 red berries + 7 milk + 8 eggs + 2 honey → 1 Normal Cake.

Reference: Palworld Wiki: Cakes. The page also contains other cake variants. Their recipes are not interchangeable with this tool’s Normal Cake recipe and are not part of the calculator.

Flour milling

3 wheat → 1 flour at a Mill.

Reference: Palworld Wiki: Flour. Milling is treated as a whole-item conversion. No time estimate or productivity multiplier is applied.

Reference date and catalog

These recipe values were checked on October 21, 2026. This is a dated recipe snapshot, not a claim of compatibility with every current platform build, future update, server rule or mod. The shareable catalog identifier is normal-cake-2026-10; the plan schema is version 1.

Before committing a large batch, compare the recipe in your own game. If an update changes an amount, the calculator needs an explicit data and test update; it does not fetch new recipes in the background.

What “craftable” means here

Capacity only means you have enough ingredients for that many whole recipes. It does not mean the cakes are already cooked, that a station is available, or that production will finish at a certain time. Suitable facilities and in-game work are still required. See the Cooking Pot reference for game context.

No breeding probabilities, timing, offspring traits or guaranteed outcomes are modeled. Spoilage, work skills, farming, transport and world settings are also outside scope.

How the figures are used

The methodology describes how ready cakes, stock deductions, whole-flour rounding and tied limiting ingredients work. The worked guide gives a reproducible example. The calculation and plan-validation code ships with the page rather than depending on a remote service.
