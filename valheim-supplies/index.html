    Small catalog, open method

    The math,
    without the mystery.

    Party target = players × amount per person. Subtract finished stock, divide the remaining need by the recipe output, then round up to whole batches. Multiply each ingredient by those batches, combine shared ingredients, and subtract raw stock once.

    Seven recipes are included: carrot soup, Queen’s jam, sausages, minor healing mead, poison resistance mead, wood arrows, and fire arrows. This is not a full Valheim database or an optimized loadout. Every recipe, source, and limitation is visible.
