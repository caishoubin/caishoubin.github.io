Plain-language privacy / 21 October 2026

Your pack stays in your browser.

Hearth & Pack is a static, client-side planner. It has no account, backend calculator, game-save access, analytics, remote fonts, or advertising scripts. Quantities are calculated on your device. We do not send your plan to an API.

Remembering a plan.

When “Remember valid edits on this device” is on, valid edits are saved to this browser’s local storage. This is not cloud sync. Clearing site data, private browsing, storage restrictions, or changing devices may remove or hide the saved plan. Turning remembering off removes this planner’s saved plan, not other tools’ data.

A valid shared link opens a preview without replacing the saved plan, even if you edit that preview. “Save this plan on device” deliberately replaces the saved plan. “Return to saved plan” discards the preview. If another tab changes the stored plan, automatic saves stop until you explicitly save your copy or return to the stored version.

Links and exports.

“Copy plan link” puts the input quantities in a versioned URL fragment after #. Browsers do not send that fragment as part of an ordinary HTTP request. It is not encryption: anyone who receives the full link, sees your history, or can access your browser can read the quantities. Do not add private information to links.

Copy and text-export actions generate a packing list locally. Clipboard access is attempted only after a copy button is pressed. If it is unavailable, the planner offers selectable text instead. Exports go through your browser’s download controls. You choose whether and where to share them.

Hosting and external links.

GitHub Pages hosts the site and may process ordinary connection data such as IP addresses and request details under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. External recipe-reference links open third-party sites with their own privacy practices.

An AdSense account-verification meta tag is present. It establishes publisher information only: it does not load ads, set advertising cookies, or mean the site has been approved. Any future advertising would need a separate privacy review.

Clear your work.

Turn off remembering to remove this tool’s saved plan, then reset the example or close the tab. Delete downloaded text files and shared links separately if you no longer want them. The reset button changes the current example; it is not an eraser for files or links already shared.
