Field note / The batch math

Round once. Gather once.

A list of desired finished supplies is not yet a crafting list. Recipes make fixed base quantities: soup makes one, jam and sausages make four, the two supported meads make six bottles after fermentation, and arrows make twenty.

1. Subtract finished stock before rounding.

Four players want two Queen’s jam each, so the party target is eight. There are three jam already available. Only five more are missing. Five divided by four is 1.25, so the plan needs two whole batches. Those batches produce eight new jam, of which three are extra after meeting the target.

Rounding every player’s demand separately would create unnecessary batches. The planner combines the party target first. If finished stock meets or exceeds the target, the required batches are zero.

2. Apply the recipe to whole batches.

Each jam batch uses eight raspberries and six blueberries. Two batches therefore require sixteen raspberries and twelve blueberries. Ingredients cannot be reduced to a fractional batch just because only five new jam were wanted.

3. Combine ingredients before subtracting stock.

Now the same four players want two minor healing mead bottles each and already own one. Seven are missing. One base ferments into six bottles, so two batches produce twelve bottles and five crafted extras. Together these mead batches use twenty honey, ten blueberries, twenty raspberries, and two dandelions.

Combined jam + healing mead: need 36 raspberries, 22 blueberries, 20 honey, and 2 dandelions. If the chest holds 10 raspberries, 5 blueberries, and 25 honey, collect 26 raspberries, 17 blueberries, and 2 dandelions. No more honey is needed.

Subtracting the blueberry stock from each recipe independently would spend the same stock twice. This planner subtracts it only after both recipes have been added together.

4. Distinguish extras from existing excess.

The table’s “Extra” column shows only newly crafted overage caused by whole-batch rounding. If you own twenty jam but need eight, no batches are made and crafted extra is zero. The twelve unused jam were already in your chest, not created by this plan.

Exactly what this calculation assumes.

All quantities are non-negative whole numbers. Party size is 1–100, personal targets are 0–10,000, and stock fields are 0–100,000,000. The planner uses unmodified base yields. Cooking-skill bonus output is not guaranteed and is excluded, along with fuel, construction, production time, and mods. Mead bases already made or fermenting are not modeled.
