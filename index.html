03 / A note from the maker

Small by design.
Useful by intention.

This is the project home of caishoubin: a collection of independent tools, practical guides, and things made out of curiosity. Each project has its own home, so you can bookmark the one you use and come straight back to it.

The game tools are unofficial fan projects. They do not connect to your game or read your save files. Planning results depend on each tool’s stated assumptions; game names and trademarks belong to their respective owners.

Privacy & how this page works

This homepage is a static directory. It has no account, contact form, analytics, advertising script, or application cookies, and does not read or write browser storage. Fonts and illustrations are served locally from this site.

GitHub Pages hosts the site and may process ordinary connection information, including your IP address and browser request details, under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. Following a link opens a page with its own data practices.

An AdSense account-verification meta tag is included in the source. It does not serve ads or indicate approval. No advertising is loaded by this homepage.
