About Belt & Buffer

A small, focused workbench for one of factory building’s most useful questions: where did the throughput go?

INDEPENDENT FAN TOOL · SOLID-ITEM LOGISTICS · UPDATED 20 OCT 2026

Belt & Buffer is an independent, fan-made calculator and guide site for solid-item logistics in Satisfactory. It focuses on conveyor capacity, supply versus demand, and storage reserves. It is published by caishoubin on GitHub.

What this tool is for

Use it to size parallel conveyor routes, inspect a single-route bottleneck, estimate how long an existing stockpile will last, or plan reserve stock for a bounded interruption. The accompanying field notes explain the assumptions and show the math with worked examples.

What the numbers mean

These are continuous-flow calculations using rates that you provide. They are not measurements from a save file and do not guarantee real-time in-game behavior. Belt ratings and storage slot counts are referenced to the official community wiki; the explanatory diagrams and guide text are original.

There is no recipe database, fluid simulation, train timetable, power model or automated balancing simulation. Keeping the scope small makes the formulas easier to inspect and the results easier to interpret. See sources and assumptions before applying the results to an unusual setup.

Independent and transparent

Satisfactory is a game by Coffee Stain Studios. This site is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Coffee Stain Studios. Game names are used only to identify the subject of the tool. No official game artwork is used.

The site currently has no advertising scripts or analytics. A public advertising-account ownership tag is present, but it does not serve ads or mean the site has been approved by an advertising network. Any future advertising changes should be reflected in the privacy notice.

Feedback and corrections

The publisher’s GitHub profile is the available public publisher link. This site does not operate a contact form or collect support submissions. When reporting a calculation issue through an available publisher channel, include the exact input values and the result you expected.
